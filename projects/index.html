Governance by Design in Federated Healthcare AI

A governance-oriented platform concept for inter-organizational healthcare collaboration without requiring organizations to centralize sensitive clinical data.

Research Problem

Collaborative AI can create value across organizations, but it raises concerns around participation, accountability, traceability, privacy, and verification.

Approach

Design requirements are translated into an artifact combining federated learning, permissioned participation, blockchain-based traceability, and cryptographic verification mechanisms.

Contribution

Proof-of-concept evidence about how selected governance requirements can be instantiated and evaluated within a socio-technical system.
