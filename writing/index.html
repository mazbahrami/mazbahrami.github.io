About the Writing

Some Ideas Need a Different Format

A short post is often too brief for a question I want to follow properly, while an academic paper serves a different purpose. Here I can work through an idea without forcing it into either format.

The writing often draws on academic literature, ongoing research, professional experience, and computational work, but the pieces are essays and notes rather than academic publications.

Writing Formats

What You’ll Find Here

The format changes with the question, but the aim is to make the reasoning clear and useful.

Essays

Longer pieces where I develop an argument, connect ideas, or examine a problem from more than one angle.

Notes

Shorter pieces on concepts, readings, emerging questions, and ideas that are still taking shape.

Data and Methods
