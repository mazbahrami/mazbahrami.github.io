Writing

Scientific Essays, Research Notes, and Interdisciplinary Reflections

A space for writing about artificial intelligence, organizations, Information Systems, technology and society, and computational research methods.

Purpose

Writing Across Research and Practice

I use this section for ideas that benefit from more room than a short professional post but do not need to take the form of a journal article. The aim is to make research questions, conceptual arguments, methodological choices, and practical observations accessible without simplifying away their complexity.

Topics will often emerge from ongoing research, teaching and conference discussions, computational experiments, or reflections on how organizations encounter new technologies.

Core Themes

What I Write About

AI and Organizations

Governance, adoption, accountability, organizational capabilities, and the changing role of human judgment.

Information Systems and Design

Socio-technical systems, Design Science Research, digital infrastructures, and governance by design.

Technology and Society

How digital technologies interact with institutions, behaviour, cognition, and wider social contexts.

Data and Computational Methods

Machine learning, NLP, causal analysis, network methods, visualization, and reproducible research practice.

Editorial Principles

Rigorous, Accessible, and Transparent

Evidence First

Distinguish established evidence, interpretation, open questions, and personal reflection.

Visual Explanation

Use diagrams and data visualizations when they clarify reasoning—not merely as decoration.

Open Methods

Link to code, data, references, and methodological detail whenever appropriate and ethically possible.
