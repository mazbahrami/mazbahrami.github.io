Editorial principles

Rigorous, accessible, and transparent.

Evidence first

Distinguish established evidence, interpretation, and personal reflection.

Visual explanation

Use diagrams and data visualizations when they clarify reasoning—not merely as decoration.

Open methods

Link to code, data, and methodological details whenever appropriate and ethically possible.
