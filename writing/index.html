The Missing Control Loop: From the Boardroom to the Algorithm

Why AI governance must become a living organizational capability rather than a collection of promises.

The essay argues that the central challenge of AI governance is not a shortage of principles, committees, or disclosures. It is the absence of a reliable control loop that carries responsibility from the boardroom into organizational processes and technical architecture—and returns operational evidence to decision-makers.
