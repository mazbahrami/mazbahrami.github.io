My academic and professional path developed across several fields rather than a single disciplinary track. Engineering established a foundation in modelling and systems thinking; finance and business shifted my attention toward organizational decisions and uncertainty; professional roles in consulting, digital commerce, analytics, and blockchain brought those questions into practice.
