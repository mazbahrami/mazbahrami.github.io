A Professional Journey Through Engineering, Consulting, Digital Commerce, and Data

Before—and alongside—academic research, I worked across technical, commercial, and analytical roles. The common thread was learning how evidence becomes action inside organizations.

Professional Perspective

Understanding Technology From the Inside of Organizations

My professional path did not follow a single function or industry. I began with computational engineering, moved into consulting and market research, worked in digital commerce and large-scale retail analytics, and later gained hands-on exposure to blockchain development.

Across these settings, I saw how decisions are shaped by incomplete data, competing priorities, organizational routines, and the need to communicate across technical and managerial boundaries. That experience now informs the questions I bring to research on AI governance, cybersecurity, and digital transformation.
