Profile

A Path Across Disciplines and Professional Practice

My path began in engineering, moved through finance and business, and developed through years of professional work in analysis, consulting, digital commerce, and data. Each stage changed the questions I ask: not only whether a technology works, but how it fits into organizations, decisions, responsibilities, and everyday practice.

Today, I bring those perspectives into Information Systems research. My work connects organizational theory, AI governance, cybersecurity, system design, and computational methods to examine how emerging technologies can be useful, accountable, and resilient in real settings.
