Background

A Path Across Disciplines and Professional Practice

My path began in engineering, moved through finance and business, and developed through professional work in simulation, consulting, digital commerce, data analysis, and blockchain. Each stage changed the questions I ask: not only whether a technology works, but how it fits into organizations, decisions, responsibilities, and everyday practice.

Today, I bring those perspectives into Information Systems research. My work connects organizational theory, AI governance, cybersecurity, system design, and computational methods.
